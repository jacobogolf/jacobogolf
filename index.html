02 — Swing analysis

Two angles. One clear plan.

What to send · What you receive

Film it right. I’ll do the rest.

01

Down the line

Slow motion, camera at hand height, around 3–4 metres away, pointing towards your hands at address. Full body and club visible throughout.

02

Face on

Slow motion, camera at hand height, around 3–4 metres away. Full body and the complete swing visible.

03

Your analysis

You receive a personalised video going through your swing step by step and explaining the root cause behind the problem you are seeing.

04

Your drills

You receive a second video of me demonstrating what to do with your body and club, plus the drills I want you to use in your next practice sessions.

Before payment, I check that your videos are good enough to analyse. Once everything is clear, you complete payment and I get to work.
