01 — Selected work

High-resolution numerical modelling.

My work combines atmospheric modelling, urban climate, Earth Observation and data-driven methods to represent complex environments at high resolution and evaluate how cities respond to heat and adaptation strategies.

Numerical modelling · Climate adaptation

From city geometry to climate response.

High-resolution WRF simulations with urban canopy and building-energy models to study heatwaves, urban heat, thermal comfort and adaptation. The Grenoble scene visualises the model grid and 3D urban representation used to connect city-scale morphology with atmospheric processes.

03 — About

Physics, computation & environmental systems.

Environmental physicist · PhD
Université Grenoble Alpes

I work where physics, computation and data meet.

My core expertise is in high-resolution atmospheric and urban climate modelling, climate adaptation, Earth Observation and scientific computing. During my PhD at Université Grenoble Alpes, I developed modelling workflows and open-source tools to connect urban morphology, heat exposure, thermal comfort and building energy demand.

My broader technical background includes machine learning, geospatial analysis, HPC and reproducible research software. I have worked across academia and industry, including Université Grenoble Alpes, UNSW Sydney, GMV Aerospace & Defence and Deloitte Spain, and taught machine learning for environmental fluid mechanics at Master’s level.
